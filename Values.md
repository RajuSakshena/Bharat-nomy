\# VALUES: Decision Rules & Non-Negotiables

\#\# Ethical Foundations & Non-Negotiables  
\- Hard Lines (Absolute Zero-Tolerance Rules):  
  \- Zero fabricated data points or uncited empirical claims.  
  \- Zero partisan alignment, political bias, or political sponsorship.  
  \- Zero compromise on primary data privacy, anonymity, and participant consent.

\#\# Trade-off Heuristics (Priority Equations)  
\- Rule 1: Depth of Verification \> Speed of Publish — Thorough empirical validation and fact-checking always override release deadlines.  
\- Rule 2: Academic Rigor & Technical Depth \> Accessibility & Simplicity — Analytical precision must never be watered down or oversimplified for brevity.  
\- Rule 3: Non-Partisan Public Utility \> Commercial Profitability — Public trust, neutrality, and civic value take precedence over financial monetization.

\#\# Decision-Making Framework  
\- Conflict Resolution Order: Empirical Accuracy \-\> Non-Partisan Neutrality \-\> Citizen & Stakeholder Utility \-\> Commercial Viability  
\- Quality Gates (Mandatory Output Pre-requisites):  
  1\. Rigorous Fact-Checking: 100% data verification with explicit primary/secondary source citations.  
  2\. Linguistic Accessibility: Clear, structured, and understandable presentation without sacrificing technical rigor.  
  3\. Actionable Stakeholder Utility: Direct relevance and strategic value for targeted citizens, market actors, or national policymakers.
