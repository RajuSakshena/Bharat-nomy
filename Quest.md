\# QUEST: Strategic Mission

\#\# North Star Statement  
\- Core Mission: Democratize high-rigor, home-grown research, datasets, and insights to empower Indian citizens and market actors to make informed political and economic decisions while holding the government accountable.

\#\# Strategic Hypothesis  
\- Core Bet: We believe that non-partisan, systems-level research combining law, economics, policy, and finance will break media-driven misinformation and improve governance/market efficiency because siloed analysis fails to capture complex socio-economic realities.  
\- Counter-Thesis: While peers do not inherently set out to be partisan or insular, structural incentives in media and academia default research toward partisan capture or inaccessible paywalls unless intentionally designed for public utility and open access.

\#\# Urgency & Stakes  
\- Catalyst (Why Now?): Widespread democratic backsliding, weaponized social media misinformation, and growing public/youth frustration with governance inefficiencies create an urgent demand for unbiased, evidence-based truth.  
\- Success State: Transparent datasets and rigorous policy insights are publicly accessible, driving high-rigor media discourse, efficient market investment, and policy accountability.  
\- Failure State (Default Outcome): Further entrenchment in an unfavorable equilibrium dominated by mediocrity, systemic corruption, identity politics, and intellectual stagnation.

\#\# Guiding Inquiry  
\- Central Research Question: How can an independent, interdisciplinary research framework deliver accessible, non-partisan evidence that counters misinformation, optimizes market decisions, and improves public governance accountability in India?
